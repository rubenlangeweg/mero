# Artwork credits

The public demo includes three images from The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Each work was marked `isPublicDomain: true` in the museum’s Collection API when checked on 25 October 2026. The Met makes its Open Access images available under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). See [The Met’s Open Access policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access).

## Image sources

- **Wheat Field with Cypresses:** [museum JPEG](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ep/original/DP-42549-001.jpg), [API record](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/436535). Purchase, The Annenberg Foundation Gift, 1993. Bundled as `van_gogh_wheat_field.webp`.
- **Women Picking Olives:** [museum JPEG](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ep/original/DP-17161-001.jpg), [API record](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/436536). The Walter H. and Leonore Annenberg Collection, Gift of Walter H. and Leonore Annenberg, 1995, Bequest of Walter H. Annenberg, 2002. Bundled as `van_gogh_olive_pickers.webp`.
- **Under the Wave off Kanagawa:** [museum JPEG](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/as/original/DP130155.jpg), [API record](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/45434). H. O. Havemeyer Collection, Bequest of Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer, 1929. Bundled as `hokusai_great_wave.webp`.

The bundled files preserve the museum photographs’ aspect ratios and are resized and encoded as WebP, at most 2560 × 1440 pixels. No artwork content has been added or removed from these files. The app’s Fill setting crops the display to the TV screen; Fit shows the complete image.

The application’s code license is separate from the image rights above. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Met.
